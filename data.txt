         THE GOLDEN FORK RESTAURANT - COMPLETE INFORMATION
================================================================
-----------
The Golden Fork is a fine dining restaurant located at 45 Maple
Avenue, Downtown Chicago, Illinois. Established in 2010 by renowned
chef Marco Bianchi, the restaurant has built a stellar reputation
for offering an exceptional blend of Italian and Continental cuisine
in an elegant, welcoming atmosphere.

The restaurant operates seven days a week, from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM
on weekdays and 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM on weekends, offering brunch,
lunch, and dinner services. It accommodates up to 150 guests across
two beautifully decorated dining halls and a private banquet room
available for special events and corporate gatherings.

The menu at The Golden Fork features a wide variety of dishes,
including freshly made pasta, grilled seafood, wood-fired pizzas,
and an impressive selection of vegetarian options. Their signature
dish, the Truffle Mushroom Risotto, has been featured in Chicago
Food Magazine three years in a row. The average cost per person is
approximately $35 to $60, and the restaurant accepts all major
credit cards, digital wallets, and cash.

The Golden Fork holds a 4.8-star rating on Google Reviews, praised
for its warm service, cozy ambiance with dim lighting and live piano
music every Friday and Saturday evening, and its carefully curated
wine list featuring over 80 international labels. Reservations can
be made via their website at www.goldenforkchicago.com or by calling
+1 (312) 555-0198. Walk-ins are welcome, subject to availability.

The restaurant also offers takeaway and delivery services through
its own platform and third-party apps. A loyalty program rewards
regular customers with exclusive discounts and early access to
seasonal menus.
